About Us

Moving Cargo,
Building Trust

VL Global Impex is a full-service import & export company headquartered in Kolar Gold Fields, Karnataka. With branches in Bangalore and Chennai, we are positioned to serve businesses across South India and beyond.

We specialize in customs clearance, freight forwarding, warehousing, and transportation — delivering seamless end-to-end logistics with full regulatory compliance. Every shipment is handled with precision, speed, and accountability.
